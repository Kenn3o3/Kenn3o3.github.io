Under review at the **International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR 2027)**. Preprint: [arXiv:2609.36588](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36588)

The paper studies reinforced fine-tuning for cooperative multi-agent vision-language-action models. The pipeline has three stages: initialization-aware data collection, offline credit-filtered tuning, and online latent-space reinforcement learning. It is evaluated with π₀ and π₀.₅ on RoboTwin, RoboFactory, and real-world manipulation with two Franka robots.
